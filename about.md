## Where I'm coming from

I'm finishing my law degree at Swinburne University of Technology, and I work as a law clerk at
YourLawyer, where I work with senior partners across a range of matters. Alongside that I spent
over a year as a volunteer Legal Intake Officer at South-East Monash Legal Service, sitting in
with lawyers as they gave clients preliminary advice on everything from VCAT and Magistrates'
Court matters to torts, crime and civil disputes, and drafting the briefs that came out of those
conversations.

Most of what I know about advocacy came from mooting. In 2026 I was selected as team leader for
one of two Swinburne teams at the RMIT Junior Criminal Law Moot, where we won every preliminary
round and reached the semi-finals. A few weeks later I appeared as Senior Counsel in the Swinburne
Senior Moot with my teammate Nicola Burgess, arguing for the prosecution in front of two KCs and
our Dean, and we won. Preparing final submissions in 20 minutes between rounds taught me more
about structure under pressure than any amount of reading.

I also tutor IT, and I volunteer as an advocacy tutor for Swinburne's contract law moot. That is
really where this site started: I kept writing the same guidance out for different groups of
students, so I turned it into something anyone could use.

## Why these tools exist

Citation formatting and moot preparation take hours that should go into the actual argument. Every
tool here is free, runs entirely in your browser, and sends nothing anywhere. I use them myself,
which is the only reason they cover the awkward cases.

If you spot something wrong, or there's a tool you wish existed, I'd like to hear about it.
